I am a Research Scientist and Leccturer at the Institute of Applied Geosciences, TU Darmstadt, working at the intersection of geomechanics, computational modeling, geophysics and structural geology. My research focuses on understanding the complex interactions between geological structures, fluid flow, stress evolution and seismicity in the Earth's crust. By integrating geological observations, geophysical observations and advanced numerical simulations, I aim to develop a better understanding of how fractured reservoirs respond to natural and anthropogenic perturbations.

My research contributes to the development of safe and sustainable subsurface technologies supporting the energy transition, including geothermal energy, CO₂ storage, and hydrogen storage. develop physics-based models to investigate fracture-controlled fluid migration, stress redistribution, and seismic hazard in complex geological environments.
