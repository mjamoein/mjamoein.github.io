I am a Lecturer and DFG Principal Investigator at the Institute of Applied Geosciences, TU Darmstadt, working at the intersection of geomechanics, computational modeling, geophysics and structural geology. My research focuses on understanding how geological structures, fluid flow, stress evolution and seismicity interact within the Earth's crust. My work is application-oriented and addresses scientific and engineering challenges related to the sustainable use of the subsurface. I use advanced multiphysics simulations to investigate the physical processes governing subsurface systems. The methodologies I develop are broadly applicable to a wide range of geohazards associated with both natural geological processes, such as earthquakes, fault reactivation, and engineered subsurface systems, including geothermal energy, carbon storage, hydrogen storage and other geotechnical engineering application.

I began my academic journey with my Bachelor’s degree in Petroleum Engineering from Amirkabir University of Technology, Iran (2006–2010), followed by a Master’s degree in Reservoir Geoscience and Engineering from IFP School, France (2010–2012). Then, I completed my PhD in Earth Sciences at ETH Zurich (2015–2018), where I studied the relationship between fracture networks, stress heterogeneities, and injection-induced seismicity in deep geothermal reservoirs. During my postdoctoral research in Germany, I expanded my research toward coupled thermo-hydro-mechanical processes in various geotechnical applications. Currently, as a Research Scientist and DFG Principal Investigator at TU Darmstadt, I am actively involved in both research and teaching activities within the Engineering Geology group.

I welcome motivated BSc and MSc students interested in geomchanics and numerical modeling. I actively support and mentor students throughout their research projects, providing training in multiphysics modeling, numerical simulation and the interpretation of coupled geological processes. My goal is to help students develop strong research skills, gain hands-on experience with state-of-the-art computational tools and build a solid foundation for careers in academia and industry.
